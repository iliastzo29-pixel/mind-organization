# 🎨 Design

The look of the vault: dark navy cards, lavender headings, one gold accent, and everything that moves is drawn in code (no stock videos).

- **Start page:** a brain drawn in WebGL, six circles that open the areas of the vault.
- **Control Room:** a header with particle waves, the goal with a live countdown, My Knowledge, customers and competitors that scroll past.
- **My Daily:** neon waves behind the Personal Assistant.
- **My Economy:** falling numbers behind the Finance Manager.

Open the start page from the command palette: **Mind Organization: Open the start page**.
