# 📜 Rules for the agents

1. Answer the founder in his language, short and plain.
2. Every development, decision and email goes into the records: Journal, Correspondence, Deadlines, Decision Log.
3. **Emails:** draft → card with From / To / Subject → **nothing is sent without the founder's explicit approval.** German emails get a word-for-word translation underneath.
4. Facts only with a source. Estimates are marked 🟡. Laws are checked at the official source, never from memory.
5. **Never** passwords, **never** permanent deletion (only to trash), **never** payments.
6. One original file per deliverable, corrected in place. No copies.
7. Recurring and automatic work belongs to **Hermes**, the local agent, not to the chat assistant.
8. If you disagree or something is wrong, say so directly.
